## A. Originality review

### Overall finding

The evidence puts the game closer to category **C (potentially problematic near-identical recreation)** than a clean A finding. There are independent digital additions and different presentation, but the inherited design cluster is unusually specific. The user reports an organizer clarification accepting inspiration in general; the actual written clarification and approval of this particular combination were not supplied. Obtain a design-specific review using the table below. Do not sign an unconditional originality declaration yet.

No copied Salem illustrations, historical Town Hall character set, logo, scanned rulebook, or verbatim full rulebook was identified in the inspected runtime. This limited negative finding does not establish legal originality or rights to every asset. Distinctive mechanics and their combined experience remain a competition originality issue even where legal protection of mechanics may differ from protected expression.

The game is not yet a verified competition submission: originality approval, rights records, upstream dependency risk handling, physical mobile/browser evidence, runtime packaging, reviewer access and submission materials are open.

5. Produce runtime-only ZIP, choose screenshots, record demo, complete team fields and declarations, push exact release commit and grant reviewer access. Match all artifacts to that release and retain Final Submit confirmation.

No destructive Git reset, history rewrite, date alteration, deployment, or voice work occurred. Baseline history and `version 0.2.0.zip`/`recap.md` remain intact. Audit artifacts contain no participant reconnect tokens or private production credentials.
