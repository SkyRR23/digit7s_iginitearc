Music & sound credits

External music and recordings used in Ashfall Council. These are not original compositions by our team. Verified 31 October 2026.

Music attribution

“Phoenix (2026)” by Scott Buckley — released under CC-BY 4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au. Modifications: loudness adjustment, resampling, Ogg/MP3 encoding and a circular two-second loop crossfade.

Day: “Town in ruins loop” by Brandon Morris. Lobby: “Medieval: The Old Tower Inn”; Night: “Fantasy: Rising Moon”; results: “Medieval: Victory Theme” and “Medieval: Defeat Theme” by RandomMind. Card/table foley: Kenney and Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero). Page flip: yd. Wood creak: laleksic. Environments and church bell: Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank.com. These are CC0; source links are below. Backgrounds are normalized with loop seams edited; conclusions are eight and nine seconds. Supernatural paper swells reverse recorded shuffles.
