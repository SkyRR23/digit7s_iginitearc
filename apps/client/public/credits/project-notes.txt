## Table prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background texture for a live browser
card game, 16:9 landscape. Create a richly rendered cinematic late-1600s
candlelit council gaming table from a high, nearly top-down camera. A broad
elliptical antique dark walnut table with fine slim brass inlay rim, filling
almost the whole frame; center is uninterrupted deep burgundy damask velvet,
with elegant subtle botanical pattern and warm vignette. Candlelight at the
extreme corners, old books and parchment partly cropped beyond the rim, minimal
subtle candles and brass candlesticks. Dark warm amber edges, velvet center has
even low contrast so foreground live UI is readable. Center 85% must be clean
empty burgundy velvet. NO cards, NO people, NO portraits, NO icons, NO text, NO
letters, NO UI, NO counters, NO symbols, NO watermark. Polished AAA historical
board game environment, tactile material details, realistic painterly look,
art-directed elegant golden candle glow, balanced symmetrical composition. The
empty velvet playing surface is the important subject; props only on outermost edges.

## Portrait atlas prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: six-character portrait atlas for a
historical browser board game. A single 3-column by 2-row contact sheet, exactly
six equal rectangular panels, clean precise grid with no gutters, NO frames NO
text NO borders NO letters. Each cell is one close-up head-and-shoulders
painted-realistic portrait of a different fictional late-1600s New England
villager, centered at exactly the same scale against very dark warm brown
background. Top row: thoughtful young woman dark wavy hair black pilgrim dress;
stern middle-aged bearded man black broad brim hat; wary young woman gray linen
bonnet. Bottom row: gray-bearded older man black pilgrim hat; auburn-haired young
woman white linen bonnet; clean-shaven young man dark coat. Elegant cinematic
realistic oil-paint game art, detailed faces, warm amber candle sidelight,
subdued dark brown black gold palette, direct or slight three-quarter view.
Face centered high enough that each cell crops cleanly to circle. Consistent
professional quality, serious mystery atmosphere, no horror gore, no extra
faces, no hands or props. Exact regular 3 by 2 grid layout is essential.
